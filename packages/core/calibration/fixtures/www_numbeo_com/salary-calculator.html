International Salary Calculator

Calculate Your Take-Home Salary Worldwide

Estimate your net salary after taxes, social contributions, and mandatory deductions in over 90 countries. Our salary calculator helps you understand what you'll actually take home from your gross salary, accounting for:

  • Income taxes (national, regional, and municipal where applicable)
  • Social security and pension contributions
  • Health insurance deductions
  • Mandatory bonuses and allowances (13th/14th month salaries, vacation bonuses)
  • Regional tax variations within countries

Whether you're considering a job offer abroad, planning a relocation, or simply want to understand your current salary breakdown, this tool provides transparent calculations based on current tax regulations and employment laws.
