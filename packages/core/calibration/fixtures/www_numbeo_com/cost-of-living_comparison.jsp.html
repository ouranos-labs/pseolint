Cost of Living Comparison

Thinking about moving? See how far your money goes in different cities.

Numbeo's Cost of Living Comparison tool lets you compare the affordability of two cities side-by-side. Just enter the two city names and see how everyday expenses like groceries, restaurants, and rent differ. We also provide a Cost of Living Index to give you a general idea of how much cheaper or more expensive one city is overall.

This easy-to-use tool helps you:

  • Budget effectively: Understand how much you'll need to spend on essentials in different locations.
  • Make informed decisions: Compare costs to choose the city that best fits your lifestyle and finances.
  • Optimize your spending: Explore how your purchasing power changes depending on where you live.
