About Ulta Beauty

Ulta Beauty is a vast beauty retailer offering over 25,000 products from 500 brands, including exclusive lines like Kylie Cosmetics and CHANEL. The Salon at Ulta Beauty provides various services, including hair, skin, and makeup services, as well as ear piercing. Conscious Beauty at Ulta Beauty caters to customers with specific preferences, offering vegan, clean ingredient, cruelty-free, and sustainable packaging options. In-store, customers can expect a wide selection of products, including hard-to-find and mainstream brands. Although the store layout may feel overwhelming to some, the friendly and helpful staff are known for their excellent customer service. Even during peak holiday seasons, they maintain smiles and a positive attitude. The salon services, such as eyebrow waxing, receive high praise for their quality and comfort. Customers have shared experiences of receiving personalized help, leading to improved makeup and skincare routines. Overall, Ulta Beauty is a go-to destination for beauty enthusiasts, with its vast product range and exceptional customer service.
