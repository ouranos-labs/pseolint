Services offered by 1Password

1Password understands that different users have unique needs when it comes to password management. To cater to this, they offer a range of plans designed to provide personalized solutions. Whether you are an individual, a family, or part of a team or business, 1Password has a plan for you.

Personal & Family Plan

The Personal & Family plan is perfect for individuals and families looking for secure and convenient password management. With this plan, you can enjoy the benefits of 1Password for only $2.99 per month when billed annually. It provides all the essential features you need to keep your passwords organized and accessible.

If you have a larger family and want to include up to five members, 1Password offers a Family plan priced at $4.99 per month. This plan allows you to share passwords and documents securely, ensuring that everyone in your family can enjoy the benefits of 1Password.

Team and Business Solution

For businesses and teams, 1Password offers three main plans: Teams, Business, and Enterprise. These plans provide advanced features and enhanced security measures to meet the specific needs of organizations.

The Teams plan is ideal for small businesses and teams with up to 50 members. It offers secure password sharing, document storage, and access controls, allowing teams to collaborate effectively while maintaining data security.

The Business plan is suitable for larger organizations, providing additional features such as advanced reporting, security monitoring, and custom roles and permissions. This plan ensures that businesses have complete control over their password management and can efficiently manage user access and permissions.

For enterprises with complex password management requirements, 1Password offers the Enterprise plan. This plan provides advanced security features, including additional integrations and enterprise-level support, to meet the needs of large organizations with multiple teams and departments.

With their comprehensive range of plans, 1Password ensures that individuals, families, teams, and businesses can find the perfect password management solution tailored to their specific needs.
