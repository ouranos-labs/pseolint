        Career Information

        As previously mentioned, his career did not get started in the typical fashion. Derulo managed to get noticed for his skills with songwriting at a rather young age. His first break came when he wrote a song for Birdman, a New Orleans rapper. He also sang on the song, too, and that helped to get him some notice.

        He won an award in 2006 that helped to catapult him to success for his work on the show: Showtime at the Apollo. Following this, he had the opportunity to write songs for famous names such as Diddy and Lil Wayne while also showing his unique penchant for singing. Between his voice and his writing work, he was able to release his debut album in 2010 and was featured on the Monster Ball Tour with Lady Gaga.

        Since then, he has recorded many famous songs such as “Want to Want Me” and “Goodbye.” He continues to maintain his career and work with new artists while innovating his style.
