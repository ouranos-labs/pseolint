Rob Gomez (Actor) Wiki, Age, Wife, Family, Height, Nationality, Net Worth, BIO

Rob Gomez (born 23 March 1997, Age: 26 years old) is a Filipino actor, singer, dancer, and host. He is the son of former actress Kate Gomez and the nephew of actor Gary Estrada. He is part of the Ejercito-Estrada clan, a prominent political and acting family in the Philippines. He is currently starring in GMA Network’s Mano Po Legacy: The Family Fortune as Joseph Chan, the first-born of the Chan clan.

Early Life and Education

Rob Gomez was born on March 23, 1997, in Manila, Philippines. His full name is Robert Michael Jason Jorge B. Ejercito. He is the grandson of the late actor George Estregan and Agnes Moran. He is also the grand-nephew of former president Joseph Estrada. He grew up in San Antonio, Quezon, where he attended elementary and high school. He opted not to use the famous family name in the entertainment industry, saying that he wanted to start his own journey and not live in the shadow of anyone.

Rob has been interested in performing arts since he was a child. He joined various singing and dancing competitions in school and in his hometown. He also learned to play the guitar and the piano. He was influenced by his mother, who was also an actress and a singer. He said that his mother was his biggest supporter and inspiration.

Rob pursued his college education at the De La Salle College of Saint Benilde, where he took up export management. He said that he chose this course because he wanted to learn more about business and entrepreneurship. He also said that he wanted to have a backup plan in case his showbiz career did not work out. He graduated in 2021.

Career

Rob started his showbiz career in 2015 when he joined the late-night variety show Walang Tulugan with the Master Showman, hosted by the legendary German Moreno. He was part of the show’s talent pool, where he showcased his singing, dancing, and hosting skills. He was also batchmates with Kapuso stars Derrick Monasterio, Jake Vargas, and Ken Chan.

However, Rob decided to take a break from showbiz in 2016 to focus on his studies. He said that he wanted to finish his degree first before pursuing his passion. He also said that he wanted to improve his craft and gain more experience before returning to the limelight.

In 2021, Rob made his comeback in the movie industry, when he was offered to play the lead role in A Girl + A Guy, a romantic comedy film directed by acclaimed filmmaker Erik Matti. He played the role of Rap, a young man who struggles with his relationship and career. He co-starred with Alexandra Trese, a model and influencer who played the role of Fiona, Rap’s love interest. The film was released on UPSTREAM.ph, a digital streaming platform, on June 24, 2021.

Rob received positive reviews for his performance in the film, which showcased his acting range and versatility. He also gained popularity and recognition for his good looks and charm. He said that he felt blessed and grateful for the opportunity to work with Direk Erik, whom he considered as the greatest director in the country. He also said that he learned a lot from his co-star Alexandra, who was also his friend and mentor.

In 2022, Rob appeared in an episode of Regal Studio Presents, a weekend anthology series produced by Regal Entertainment. He played the role of Lester, a young man who falls in love with his best friend’s girlfriend. He co-starred with Kim Domingo and Jeric Gonzales, who played the roles of Rose and Brody, respectively.

In 2023, Rob landed his first television series, Mano Po Legacy: The Family Fortune, a drama series based on the popular Mano Po film franchise by Regal Films. He played the role of Joseph Chan, the first-born son of the wealthy and influential Chan family. However, due to his illegitimate status, he is not recognized by his father and his siblings. He co-starred with veteran actors Christopher de Leon, Alice Dixson, and Sunshine Dizon, who played the roles of his father, stepmother, and half-sister, respectively.

Personal Life and Controversies

Rob is in a relationship with Shaila Rebortera, a British-Filipino dentist and beauty queen. She is the winner of Miss Multinational Philippines 2021 and Miss Millennial Philippines 2018. She also placed second runner-up in Reyna ng Aliwan 2018 and runner-up in Miss Cebu 2016. She hails from Talisay City, Cebu.

Rob and Shaila have a daughter named Amelia, who was born in December 2022. They kept their relationship and their daughter a secret from the public for over a year until Shaila revealed them on her Instagram account on August 4, 2023. She said that she decided to come out with the truth because there was no such thing as a perfect time. She also said that she wanted to show her daughter to the world and to show the world to her daughter.

However, a day after Shaila’s revelation, she posted photos of her bruises on her Instagram stories, implying that she was physically abused by Rob. She also hinted that Rob was keeping their relationship a secret for his career and that his mother asked her to apologize for the incident. She said that she left him after over a year of being silent about the abuse.

Rob has not yet issued any statement regarding Shaila’s allegations. His fans and supporters have expressed their shock and disbelief over the issue, while Shaila’s friends and fellow beauty queens have shown their support and sympathy for her.

Net Worth

Rob’s net worth is estimated to be around $1 million, as of 2023. He earns his income from his acting projects, endorsements, and appearances. He is also a co-owner of a restaurant in Quezon City, which he opened with his friends in 2021.

Social Media

Rob is active on social media, where he has a large following. He has over 500,000 followers on Instagram, where he posts photos and videos of his personal and professional life. He also has over 100,000 followers on Facebook, where he shares updates and news about his projects and events.
