Our Story
Simplicity, efficiency and connection to our customers is at the core of our practices and beliefs

It all started with 2 partners who were both in need of real, authentic customer reviews.

An idea was born to partner up and deliver a service that would conveniently and constantly drive real reviews for local businesses across US and Canada.

After in depth research one thing was clear: local businesses were left behind the innovation curve.

There was a need of a centralized hub that would unite all essential small business software tools & marketing automation at an easily affordable cost. Customers of local business should win from convenience and in return, small business owners could focus on delivering more of their great products and services.
