Trichy DHS Recruitment 2026 | Salary Rs 17500 to 63000 | Last Date – 19/06/26 | Tamilnadu Govt Jobs 2026

Trichy DHS Officially Released this Notification. Trichy Officials are inviting applications from the eligible and interested candidate. Tamilnadu Govt Jobs 2026 Educational Qualification, Age Limit, Salary, Selection Process, How to Apply…etc

Post Name

  1. Medical Officer – 01 Post
  2. Health Visitor(TB) – 01 Post
  3. Data Entry Operator – 01 Post

Educational Qualification

  1. Medical Officer – Must have completed an undergraduate medical degree (MBBS) and be registered with the Medical Council. Diploma / M.D. in Public Health / P.S.M (Preventive and Social Medicine) / Community Medicine / CHA (Community Health Administration) / Tuberculosis & Chest Diseases. 1 year of experience in NTEP (National Tuberculosis Elimination Program).
  2. Health Visitor(TB) – Must have passed a Bachelor’s degree in Science. Intermediate (10+2) in science. Experience of working as MPW (Multi-Purpose Health Worker) / LHV (Lady Health Visitor) / ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife) / HW (Health Worker) / Education / Counselling OR a recognized Tuberculosis Health Visitors Course. Certificate Course in Computer Operation (Minimum 2 months).
  3. Data Entry Operator – Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science OR a Diploma in Computer Applications (DCA) / Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applications (PGDCA). Government-recognized certificate in Tamil and English Typewriting.

Age Limit

  1. Medical Officer – 59 Years
  2. Health Visitor(TB) –  59 Years
  3. Data Entry Operator –  59 Years

  2. Health Visitor(TB) – Rs.15000/-
  3. Data Entry Operator – Rs.17500/-
