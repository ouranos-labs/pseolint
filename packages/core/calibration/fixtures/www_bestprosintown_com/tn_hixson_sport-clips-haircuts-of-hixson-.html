About Sport Clips Haircuts of Hixson

Sport Clips Haircuts of Hixson, TN, offers a unique experience for men and boys, blending sports on TV with skilled hairstyling. Customers enjoy the legendary steamed towel treatment and leave feeling like an MVP. Many appreciate the quality cuts and kindness of the stylists, with some praising the personal attention and professionalism of staff like Emily and Samantha. Samantha, a new stylist, is especially recognized for her patience, attention to detail, and ability to deliver exactly what clients want, even after long wait times. Amanda is also highlighted for her patience, kindness, and precise work, consistently meeting clients’ expectations. While some customers mention concerns about rising prices and the decrease in tipping, the online check-in system remains a helpful feature, despite some suggestions for improvements. Overall, Sport Clips is known for its good service, patience, and caring staff, making it a favored spot for haircuts in the community, especially for children with special needs.
