About Ulta Beauty

Ulta Beauty offers a wide range of products and services, including cosmetics, fragrance, body, skin, and hair care products from over 500 brands. The store provides a personalized shopping experience, with knowledgeable staff members dedicated to helping customers find the right products. Associates like Anita and Janna provide exceptional customer service, offering free samples and expert recommendations to ensure customers find what they need. The Salon at Ulta Beauty offers a variety of services, including haircuts, hair color, and highlights, with skilled stylists like Rhonda providing excellent results. Customers have praised staff members such as Tia, who is known for her personality and expertise, helping with quick and efficient shopping assistance. Walter is appreciated for his attentive approach and ability to help achieve desired hair looks while prioritizing hair protection. Maria Calvillo received high marks for her phenomenal cut and color, ensuring client satisfaction and comfort throughout the appointment. With a focus on Conscious Beauty, Ulta Beauty offers products that cater to various preferences, including vegan, clean ingredient, cruelty-free, and sustainable packaging options.
