      Charlee Fraser Quick Info
      Height 5 ft 10.5 in
      Weight 54 kg
      Date of Birth December 25, 1995
      Zodiac Sign Capricorn
      Eye Color Dark Brown

      Charlee Fraser is an Australian model who has managed to rise through the ranks in the highly competitive industry in a short span of time. Being of indigenous origin, she has also paved the way for ethnic and cultural diversity in this field. Her doe-eyed looks and freshly-cut bob hairstyle have revolutionized her image and got her noticed from the bigwigs in fashion.

      Charlee Fraser Facts

      1. She found her big break in modeling in 2013 after being discovered by a photographer in her hometown, Newcastle. She was studying beauty and science in college at that time.
      2. Her beautiful long locks were cut into a short bob by famous hairstylist Guido Palau at the behest of Alexander Wang who wanted to book her exclusively for the autumn/winter 2016 season.
      3. In 2017, she appeared in the 80’s inspired editorial for Vogue Australia.
      4. Charlee is arguably the first indigenous Australian model to cross over into the international modeling circuit.
      5. She was included in the “Top 50 Models Of The Fashion Industry” list by models.com.
      6. She was the most booked model during the New York Fashion Week spring 2018.
