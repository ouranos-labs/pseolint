About Regal Nails, Salon & Spa

Regal Nails, Salon & Spa is a family-owned business that offers a wide range of beauty services, including manicures, pedicures, acrylic nails, and waxing. Customers appreciate the relaxing atmosphere and friendly technicians who provide exceptional service with smiles. Cleanliness and safety are of utmost priority, ensuring a comfortable experience for all. Many clients have been loyal for years, enjoying the salon's affordable prices and high-quality work. Special techniques, like cat eye nails, are also available. New customers have shared their positive experiences, noting that technicians are meticulous and attentive, checking in throughout the service to ensure satisfaction. The salon has earned a reputation for excellent eyebrow services, with some clients refusing to go to anyone else. With a focus on customer satisfaction, Regal Nails, Salon & Spa is a trusted choice for individuals and families seeking pampering and beauty services.
