Most marketing teams have figured out content generation. The harder problem — the one separating teams that get real leverage from AI from teams that just get faster drafts — is content orchestration: turning a one-off prompt into a repeatable system that runs end-to-end. When that's done well, the metrics move: cycle times collapse, output scales without adding headcount, and quality stays on-brand because the knowledge is encoded into the system instead of trapped in someone's head.
