      Franko Fraize Facts

      1. He started his music career in 2007 by initially focusing on rapping about the small town he was raised in. However, he wasn’t sure if he would receive the right sort of backing to become successful.
      2. Early in his career, he focussed on uploading his music on YouTube and Myspace to gain exposure.
      3. In 2012, he was brought in by Mike Skinner to support him on the Norwich leg of The D.O.T. tour. It was a huge moment in his career as he considers Skinner as his influence.
