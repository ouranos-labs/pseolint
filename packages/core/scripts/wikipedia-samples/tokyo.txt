Tokyo, officially the Tokyo Metropolis, is the capital city of Japan and one of the most populous cities in the world. As of 2023, the Greater Tokyo Area, which includes Tokyo and its surrounding prefectures, is the most populous metropolitan area in the world, with an estimated 37.4 million residents.

Tokyo is the seat of the Japanese government and the Imperial Palace, and the city serves as Japan's economic centre. Its economy is one of the world's largest city economies, with its gross metropolitan product estimated at $2.0 trillion in 2018. Tokyo is also one of the most expensive cities in the world.

The city of Tokyo was founded in 1457 AD, when Ota Dokan built Edo Castle. Edo, as Tokyo was known until 1868, grew to become one of the largest cities in the world during the Edo period. After the Meiji Restoration, the imperial capital was moved from Kyoto to Edo, which was renamed Tokyo, meaning Eastern Capital.

Tokyo is a global financial centre and a global city. The city is home to the Tokyo Stock Exchange, one of the world's largest stock exchanges, and is a major hub for international finance. Tokyo is also home to the headquarters of many of Japan's major corporations, as well as the Japanese branches of many foreign companies.

Tokyo is known for its advanced transportation infrastructure. The city is served by an extensive network of railway lines, including the Tokyo Metro and the Toei Subway. The Shinkansen bullet train connects Tokyo to other major Japanese cities, including Osaka, Nagoya, and Kyoto. Narita International Airport and Haneda Airport serve the city's international and domestic aviation needs.

The city is home to a number of world-class museums, including the Tokyo National Museum, the National Museum of Western Art, and the Mori Art Museum. Tokyo is also home to many famous entertainment districts, including Shinjuku, Shibuya, and Akihabara.

Tokyo is known for its unique blend of traditional and modern culture. The city is home to a number of ancient temples and shrines, including the Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa and the Meiji Shrine in Harajuku. The city is also home to many cutting-edge technology companies and is known for its vibrant pop culture scene, including anime, manga, and video games.

Tokyo cuisine is renowned around the world, and the city has more Michelin-starred restaurants than any other city in the world. Tokyo is known for its sushi, ramen, tempura, and other traditional Japanese dishes, as well as its vibrant street food culture.
