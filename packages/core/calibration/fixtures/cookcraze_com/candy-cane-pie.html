Candy Cane Pie

by Sara McKenney

Save this recipe on:

  • WhatsApp
  • Facebook

When winter rolls in and peppermint takes over the season, I find myself dreaming of this decadent Candy Cane Pie. It’s not just a dessert—it’s a celebration on a plate. Creamy, cool, and bursting with minty chocolate flavor, this pie has become my go-to when I want something festive and foolproof for holiday gatherings. Every bite delivers a refreshing contrast between a rich chocolate crust and a fluffy peppermint filling, crowned with clouds of whipped cream and playful candy canes. It looks like it came from a bakery, but I promise—it’s easier than it seems.

What I love most about this pie is how it brings people together. It’s the kind of dessert that gets everyone talking: a showstopper centerpiece that tastes even better than it looks. The blend of textures, the cool peppermint zing, and the hint of nostalgia from the candy canes make it a winter favorite. Whether you serve it at a party or save it as a quiet treat for yourself, Candy Cane Pie never fails to make the season feel a little more magical.

Why You’ll Love This Candy Cane Pie

This pie checks every holiday dessert box: it’s stunning, simple, and irresistibly delicious. The chocolate cookie crust gives a sturdy and flavorful base, while the pink peppermint filling is light and creamy with just the right amount of sweetness. The whipped cream topping adds extra indulgence, and the crushed candy canes on top give a festive crunch. Best of all? No baking required—just chill and serve.

What Kind of Peppermint Should I Use?

I recommend using classic peppermint extract for that bold, clean flavor that defines this pie. Avoid mint extract—it tends to taste more like spearmint or toothpaste and won’t give you that true candy cane essence. Crushed candy canes or peppermint candies on top not only look adorable but also reinforce the flavor and add a delightful crunch.

Options for Substitutions

You can make Candy Cane Pie your own with a few easy swaps:

  • Crust: Swap the chocolate cookie crust for a graham cracker crust or even an Oreo crust for more chocolate punch.
  • Filling: Use mascarpone or cream cheese for a richer filling if you prefer it over whipped topping.
  • Topping: Skip the candy canes and sprinkle crushed chocolate chips, white chocolate curls, or holiday sprinkles instead.
  • Flavor twist: Add a dash of vanilla or almond extract for a deeper flavor profile.
  • Dairy-free? Opt for coconut whipped topping and a plant-based cream cheese alternative.

Ingredients for This Candy Cane Pie

  • Chocolate cookie crust: This creates a rich, slightly crunchy base that perfectly balances the creamy peppermint filling.
  • Whipped topping: Light and airy, it forms the bulk of the filling, giving it a mousse-like texture.
  • Sweetened condensed milk: Adds creaminess and a silky smooth texture to the filling, plus extra sweetness.
  • Peppermint extract: Just a teaspoon transforms the pie into a minty dream—don’t substitute with mint extract.
  • Red food coloring (optional): A few drops create that signature pink hue that gives the pie its festive charm.
  • Crushed candy canes: Mixed into the filling and sprinkled on top, they provide crunch and authentic peppermint flavor.
  • Whipped cream: For decorating the top, adding both style and extra indulgence.
  • Whole candy canes (optional): For garnish—stand them up in the center for a stunning centerpiece effect.

Step 1: Make the Crust

Start with your chocolate cookie crust. You can use a store-bought version or make one yourself by combining crushed chocolate cookies with melted butter, pressing the mixture into a pie pan. Chill it in the refrigerator while you prepare the filling.

Step 2: Whip the Filling

In a large mixing bowl, stir together the sweetened condensed milk and peppermint extract. Add a few drops of red food coloring and mix until evenly pink. Fold in the whipped topping gently to keep the texture light and airy.

Step 3: Fold in Crushed Candy Canes

Take about half of your crushed candy canes and fold them into the peppermint filling. This adds both crunch and bursts of peppermint flavor in every bite.

Step 4: Fill the Crust

Spoon the peppermint filling into the chilled chocolate crust, smoothing out the top with a spatula. It should be full to the brim.

Step 5: Chill Until Firm

Place the pie in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, or overnight if possible. This step is crucial to allow the filling to set and become sliceable.

Step 6: Decorate Before Serving

Once the pie is firm, top with swirls of whipped cream around the edges. Sprinkle the remaining crushed candy canes on top. For a festive touch, press whole candy canes into the center or arrange in a starburst pattern.

Step 7: Slice and Enjoy

Use a sharp knife to slice the pie, wiping between cuts for clean slices. Serve chilled, and enjoy that refreshing minty-creamy contrast with each bite.

How Long to Prepare the Candy Cane Pie

Making this pie is mostly hands-off, which is perfect for busy holiday schedules. The prep itself takes around 15 to 20 minutes. If you’re making the crust from scratch, add an extra 10 minutes. Most of the time required is for chilling.

  • Prep Time: 20 minutes (30 if making crust from scratch)
  • Chill Time: Minimum of 4 hours (overnight preferred)

You can make it a day ahead to reduce the day-of pressure, and it keeps beautifully in the fridge.

Tips for Perfect Candy Cane Pie

  • Chill everything: Start with chilled whipped topping and a cold pie crust to help the filling set faster.
  • Fold gently: When mixing the whipped topping into the filling, fold carefully to keep it fluffy.
  • Crush candy canes finely: Larger pieces can become chewy over time, while smaller bits give a better texture and prettier look.
  • Add color slowly: Red food coloring is potent—start with 1-2 drops and add more if needed.
  • Chill long enough: Don’t rush the chill time or the pie won’t slice properly.

Watch Out for These Mistakes While Cooking

  • Using mint extract instead of peppermint: This completely changes the flavor—always go with peppermint extract.
  • Overmixing the filling: This can deflate the whipped topping and make the texture dense.
  • Under-chilling: The pie needs time to firm up—don’t cut corners here.
  • Overdecorating: Too many toppings can overwhelm the smooth, light filling. Keep it simple.
  • Using warm crust: Make sure the crust is cool before filling to prevent melting the whipped mixture.

What to Serve With Candy Cane Pie?

1. Hot Chocolate

A warm mug of cocoa enhances the cool minty flavors of the pie.

2. Coffee or Peppermint Mocha

The boldness of coffee balances the sweetness beautifully.

3. Vanilla Ice Cream

A scoop on the side adds contrast and creaminess.

4. Mulled Wine or Cider

Great for holiday parties and pairs well with peppermint.

5. Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries

Adds elegance and another fruity-chocolate note to your dessert spread.

Storage Instructions

Refrigerator: Keep the pie in the refrigerator, covered loosely with plastic wrap or foil. It will stay fresh for 3–4 days.

Freezer: You can freeze Candy Cane Pie for up to 2 months. Wrap it tightly in plastic wrap, then foil. Thaw overnight in the fridge before serving.

Avoid: Don’t leave it at room temperature for long. The filling softens quickly and can become unstable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead can I make this pie?

You can make it 1–2 days in advance. Just store it in the fridge and add final decorations before serving.

Can I use fresh whipped cream instead of whipped topping?

Yes, but it should be stabilized whipped cream to prevent it from deflating.

Is this pie gluten-free?

Only if you use gluten-free cookies for the crust. Everything else is naturally gluten-free.

Can I make this pie vegan?

Absolutely! Use coconut whipped topping, dairy-free condensed milk, and a vegan crust.

Does it taste overly minty?

Not at all! It has a soft peppermint flavor that’s refreshing without being overpowering.

Conclusion

Candy Cane Pie is a merry masterpiece that’s as easy as it is impressive. With minimal prep, no baking, and loads of festive flair, it’s the perfect treat for the season. Whether you’re hosting or attending, this pie brings a delightful mix of nostalgia, cheer, and creamy peppermint joy to any table.

Candy Cane Pie

  • Author: Sara McKenney
  • Total Time: 4 hours 20 minutes
  • Yield: 10 servings
  • Diet: Vegetarian

Description

This no-bake Candy Cane Pie is the ultimate festive dessert—cool, creamy, and bursting with peppermint flavor. With its chocolate cookie crust, a fluffy peppermint pink filling, and swirls of whipped cream topped with crushed candy canes, it’s the kind of easy recipe that feels straight out of a bakery window. Perfect for quick breakfast treats, holiday parties, or when you need simple dessert ideas that still make a big impact. Whether you’re looking for easy dinner desserts, Christmas food ideas, or fun winter snacks, this peppermint pie brings cheer to every bite.

Ingredients

1 pre-made chocolate cookie crust

1 can (14 ounces) sweetened condensed milk

1 teaspoon peppermint extract

2–3 drops red food coloring (optional)

2 cups whipped topping (plus extra for garnish)

1/2 cup crushed candy canes (divided)

Whole candy canes for garnish (optional)

Instructions

1. Prepare the chocolate cookie crust by placing it in the fridge to chill while you mix the filling.

2. In a large bowl, stir together the sweetened condensed milk and peppermint extract.

3. Add 2–3 drops of red food coloring and mix until the color is evenly distributed.

4. Gently fold in the whipped topping, keeping the texture airy and light.

5. Mix in half of the crushed candy canes for peppermint crunch in every bite.

6. Pour the filling into the chilled crust and smooth the top evenly with a spatula.

7. Refrigerate the pie for at least 4 hours, or overnight for best results.

8. Before serving, decorate the pie with swirls of whipped cream around the edge.

9. Sprinkle the remaining crushed candy canes on top.

10. Garnish with whole candy canes in the center if desired. Slice, serve, and enjoy!

Notes

Start with chilled whipped topping and a cold crust to speed up the setting process.

Don’t skip the chilling time; it’s essential for a firm and sliceable pie.

If you prefer less sweetness, reduce the crushed candy canes mixed into the filling.
