Government Job FAQs

What is the Age Eligibility for Government Jobs?

Minimum 18 years old for most of the fresher government jobs. Maximum 56 years for Higher posts. Maximum age differ for post and designation wise.
Age Relaxation: 05 years for SC / ST, 03 years for OBC and plus 10 years for Persons with Disabilities.

What is the Minimum Qualification for Government Jobs?

Minimum VIII Standard (8th Class) Pass, 10th / SSLC / Matric / SSC / Xth std Pass, 10+2 / Higher Secondary / 12th class / XII std Pass, ITI, Diploma, B.E.. B.Tech, Degree (B.Sc., B.A., B. Com, BCA, BBA etc.) and Post Graduation (M.Sc., M.Com, M.A., M.Phil, MCA, MBA, M.E., M.Tech, Ph.D.)

Which Government Job is the best in India?

1. Public Sector Units – BHEL, ONGC, Coal India, IOCL, HPCL, etc. The best part about PSU jobs is working in fixed duty hours. The avg. CTC for a graduate engineer is Rs. 10,80,000/-
2. Defense Services – Army, Navy, or Coastal Guard. You can learn to be more organized in your personal life and professional life. The avg. salary for an assistant commandant per month is between Rs 56,100 to 1,77,500/-
3. Civil Services– IAS, IPS, IFS. They are considered the highest abode in the Indian Government. The avg. salary per month is Rs. 55,509/-
4. IAS Officer – Although the pressure of this role is high, the perks are fantastic. With the revision in the salary scale, the avg salary of a junior IAS officer is Rs. 56,100/-
5. IFS Officer. They are the brand ambassadors of the country. This job involves a lot of traveling and meeting with foreign diplomats. The perks are great. Avg. The salary of an IFS Officer is Rs. 60,000/-
6. IPS Officer. They maintain the law and order of the nation and are considered the real hero of the country. The perks offered are excellent. Avg. salary of an IPS Officer between Rs. 56,100 to Rs. 2,25,000/-
State Public Service Commission. The jobs offered here are similar to Civil Services. These iude MRO, Tahsildar, RTO, etc. They get paid a handsome salary. Avg. salary is Rs.50,000/-

How do I apply for Government Jobs in India?

You will apply for government jobs depending on online and offline. However, employers prefer the internet because it is easier and less time consuming for employees and employers. Before applying to any type, applicants should read the announcements carefully to avoid being put through the hiring committee.
