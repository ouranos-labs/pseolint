About Ulta Beauty

Ulta Beauty is a US-based beauty retailer offering a wide range of products from over 500 brands, including exclusive lines. The store provides a welcoming environment, with staff member Danielle praised for her professionalism, patience, and skill in transforming customers' looks. Danielle is a very talented and passionate hair stylist who gave beautiful highlights and a silk press that leaves hair feeling soft with lots of shine. Andrew is also super helpful and friendly, helping to find a great foundation with a perfect color match while keeping a budget in mind. Their customer service is amazing and the store is easily manageable to find what you need. The return process is smooth, and customers leave feeling satisfied and eager to return.
