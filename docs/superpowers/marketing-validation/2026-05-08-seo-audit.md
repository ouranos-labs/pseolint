# SEO Audit - pseolint.dev marketing pages (v0.6.3-v0.6.4)

**Date:** 2026-05-08
**Audited by:** SearchFit.ai SEO toolkit (read-only static analysis of `apps/web/src/app/`)
**Engine version on audited pages:** v0.6.3 (footer/methodology) - note: nav `layout.tsx` still hard-codes `v0.5.2` at line 106.

---

## Executive summary

**Overall SEO health: 84 / 100.**

Strong technical-SEO codebase, exceptional for a developer-tools marketing site. Schema discipline is unusually high — FAQPage, Article, Dataset, BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage, SoftwareApplication, Organization all wired correctly with `safeJsonLd` script-tag-breakout protection. Internal linking is hub-and-spoke around `/methodology` and `/tools`. External links go to authoritative primary sources. Brand voice preserved — concrete dates (March 27 2026 core update, May 7 2024 site-reputation-abuse, March 5 2024 scaled-content-abuse), specific numbers, no marketing inflation.

The 16-point gap to 100 is a small set of fixable issues, almost all on the homepage: it is a Client Component without page-level metadata, so it inherits the layout title verbatim and never gets a homepage-specific OG/Twitter description. Meta-description length compliance is the single most-repeated finding — most pages exceed Google ~160-char SERP truncation. The version chip in the nav is stale.

### Strengths
1. **Schema completeness is best-in-class** for the category. `/research/state-of-pseo-2026` ships Article + Dataset + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList in one page. `/limits`, `/tools`, `/rules`, `/symptoms`, `/pricing` all carry FAQPage. `/tools` adds CollectionPage with nested SoftwareApplication per tool.
2. **Per-page canonical URLs** computed from `env().BETTER_AUTH_URL` (resolved against `metadataBase`). No accidental localhost canonicals shipping to prod.
3. **Sitemap is hand-curated with sensible priorities** (`/research = 0.9`, `/tools = 0.9`, `/rules = 0.8`, `/symptoms = 0.8`, `/limits = 0.4`). robots.txt correctly disallows `/r/`, `/a/`, `/api/`, `/dashboard/`, `/signin`, and the OG-image generators. Sitemap is referenced from robots.txt.
4. **External-link `rel="nofollow"` discipline** — competitor pricing pages (Ahrefs, Semrush, Sitebulb, Screaming Frog, ContentKing) use `rel="nofollow"`; Google policy docs and IETF citations are dofollow. Exactly right.
5. **Content depth is substantive across every audited page.** No thin-marketing-fluff. The research page is several thousand words of original analysis with methodology, vertical breakdown, predictions, and CC BY 4.0 citation block (APA/BibTeX/Markdown) — exactly what answer engines extract.
6. **Brand voice is intact.** Zero instances of "revolutionary", "best-in-class", or "synergy" in the audited pages. Hedges are earned and explicit ("modeled estimates", "directional", "best-fit ranges" on the research page).
7. **The /methodology page is an SEO asset by itself** — open-corpus, open-runner, open-spec, dated snapshots, and the explicit "we did NOT update the ceiling on these sites to force a pass — that would be calibration laundering" passage.
8. **Heading hierarchy is clean** on every audited page. One H1, sequential H2/H3, descriptive (no "Section 1").

### Gaps (in priority order)
1. **Homepage has no page-level metadata override.** `apps/web/src/app/page.tsx` starts with `"use client"` (line 1), which forbids `export const metadata`. The whole page falls through to `layout.tsx` metadata. Title reads "pseolint — SpamBrain-proof your pSEO" (36 chars, fine) but the homepage never gets a v0.6-specific OG/Twitter description, and no homepage-specific schema fires.
2. **Stale version chip in nav** — `apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx:106` still reads `v0.5.2` while every audited page (homepage, methodology, tools, rules, pricing) advertises v0.6.x.
3. **Homepage H1 is 91 characters** (`apps/web/src/app/page.tsx:179`). Google synthesizes from the title tag, not the H1, so this will not truncate in SERPs — but the H1 is ~2x standard SERP-display length. On-brand as a thesis statement; flagging for awareness, not as a bug.
4. **`apps/web/src/app/pricing/layout.tsx` and `apps/web/src/app/pricing/page.tsx` both export `metadata`.** Next.js merges them; the page keyword-loaded version wins. Two declarations for the same route is a maintenance trap.
5. **No `Article` or `BreadcrumbList` schema on /methodology** despite its `og:type: "article"`. The structured-data signal would benefit from matching the OG hint with JSON-LD.
6. **No `SoftwareApplication` schema on the homepage.** `/tools` has it nested per-tool, but the root site itself is not marked up. For "free SpamBrain audit" query intent, a top-level `SoftwareApplication` with `applicationCategory: "SEOApplication"`, `offers.price: 0` would be natural.
7. **Title-length compliance is mixed; meta descriptions are universally over.** See per-page table.

## Internal linking analysis

**Hub-and-spoke depth: healthy.** Every audited page links to `/methodology` (the credibility hub) and at least one of `/tools`, `/rules`, `/symptoms`. The footer (in `layout.tsx`) hub-links every key surface across four columns: free tools, learn (rules / symptoms / research / leaderboard), product (pricing / limits / abuse / privacy / terms). No orphans found among the 8 audited pages.

**Anchor-text quality: high.** No "click here" or "read more" anchors. Anchors are descriptive (e.g. `/methodology` linked as `/methodology`, `links/host-section-divergence` written as the rule ID, "blind-spots spec" rather than "spec").

**Potential dead-end: /methodology.** Has only a bottom-CTA "Run an audit" button linking to /. A reader who lands on /methodology from a search result has only the global nav to discover /rules, /symptoms, or /research/state-of-pseo-2026. Adding a "see also" footer block linking /rules and /research/state-of-pseo-2026 would tighten the topical cluster.

**Cross-link to /research/state-of-pseo-2026 is footer-only.** No audited body content links to it. The homepage "By the numbers" stats section is a natural place to anchor "see the corpus methodology" pointing at the research page.

## Crawlability

- **robots.txt: correct.** Disallows `/r/`, `/a/`, `/api/`, `/dashboard/`, `/signin`, and the OG-image generators (`/opengraph-image`, `/twitter-image`, `/icon`, `/apple-icon`). Sitemap reference present. No accidental wildcards blocking marketing pages.
- **sitemap.ts: correct.** Includes all 8 audited pages with reasonable priorities. Tool/rule/symptom slugs populated dynamically from `MARKETING_TOOLS`, `MARKETING_RULES`, `MARKETING_SYMPTOMS` — no static drift.
- **No `noindex` meta tags found** on any audited page.
- **Canonical URLs: correct** on every page that exports `metadata`/`generateMetadata`. Built from `env().BETTER_AUTH_URL` so dev/staging will not accidentally canonicalise to prod and vice-versa.
- **No redirect chains visible** in the source. (Per instructions, no live HTTP audit was run against pseolint.dev.)

## Mobile / responsive

Tailwind classes used appropriately throughout: `max-w-3xl` (long-form pages like methodology, limits, research), `max-w-5xl` (hubs and homepage), `sm:` / `md:` / `lg:` breakpoints applied to hero, comparison tables, and stat grids. Tables wrapped in `overflow-x-auto` on /tools, /research/state-of-pseo-2026, /pricing. Touch-target sizes (`h-11` on primary buttons, `h-10` on secondary actions) are above the 44 px iOS minimum. Viewport meta is implicit from Next.js.

## Recommended fixes (ordered by impact)

### 1. Homepage page-level metadata (Critical, ~30 min)
Move the audit form into a Client Component (e.g. `apps/web/src/components/landing/audit-form.tsx`) and convert `apps/web/src/app/page.tsx` into a Server Component that exports `generateMetadata`. Inject the form via `<AuditForm />`. Add a v0.6-specific OG/Twitter description and the `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD.

### 2. Trim every meta description to <=160 chars (Important, ~20 min)
Concrete targets, with current to suggested length and brand-voice-preserving copy:

- **/methodology** (207 to 134): "How pseolint calibrates verdicts against in-production pSEO sites that win in search. Open-source corpus, runner, regression tests."
- **/symptoms** (186 to 154): "A triage list for programmatic-SEO sites that have been hit. Match the symptom in Search Console, then run a v0.6 template-aware audit to confirm."
- **/tools** (226 to 149): "Three free SEO tools backed by the open-source pseolint v0.6 engine: SpamBrain checker, thin-content scanner, doorway-page detector. No signup."
- **/rules** (269 to 143): "32 SpamBrain + AEO rules across 8 categories. Each rule aggregates to a per-template verdict in v0.6. Five flagship rules written up in depth."
- **/pricing** (226 to 137): "Free unlimited audits with K=10 per template. Pro 19 USD/mo: K=20 re-audits, monitoring, AI triage, GSC, rule overrides. No credit card."
- **/research/state-of-pseo-2026** (253 to 148): "Modeled estimates of programmatic-SEO health in 2026: failure rates across 8 SpamBrain rules, vertical and tech-stack breakdowns. CC BY 4.0."

### 3. Update the version chip in the global nav (Important, 1 line)
`apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx:106` — change `v0.5.2` to `v0.6.4`, or wire it to a build-time constant from `package.json` so it cannot drift.

### 4. Trim two over-length titles (Important, ~5 min)
- **/tools** (74 to 47): "Free SEO tools for programmatic SEO · pseolint"
- **/research/state-of-pseo-2026** (78 to 65): "State of pSEO 2026 — SpamBrain risk in programmatic SEO · pseolint"

### 5. Add `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD to /methodology (Nice-to-have, ~15 min)
Mirror the pattern from /research/state-of-pseo-2026. The methodology page already has the right shape (dated, authored by Ouranos Labs, last-calibrated metadata).

### 6. Delete redundant metadata in `apps/web/src/app/pricing/layout.tsx` (Nice-to-have, ~2 min)
The page-level metadata in pricing/page.tsx is the keyword-loaded version you want. The layout-level export risks future drift.

### 7. Add `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD to homepage (Nice-to-have, ~10 min after fix #1)

### 8. Cross-link /research/state-of-pseo-2026 from at least one body section (Nice-to-have, ~5 min)
Natural anchor: the homepage "By the numbers" stats list, or the methodology page "Trade-offs" section.

## What is actually GOOD (resist the urge to change)

- **The brand voice.** Specific dates, version numbers, hedge language. Do not let any rewrite (including the description trims above) collapse "the March 27, 2026 core update that tightened scaled-content signals on date-stacked corpora" into "recent Google updates".
- **The /methodology page "we did NOT update ceilings" passage.** Single most credibility-building sentence in the codebase. Keep it.
- **The /tools comparison table** with real list prices for Ahrefs / Semrush / Sitebulb / Screaming Frog / ContentKing. The kind of page LLM-powered SERPs cite for "alternatives to Ahrefs site audit" queries.
- **The research page CC BY 4.0 + APA/BibTeX/Markdown citation block.** Optimised for academic and LLM citation; almost no marketing site does this.
- **`safeJsonLd()` defensive escaping** on every JSON-LD insertion. Do not let anyone "simplify" it away.
- **External-link `rel="nofollow"` discipline** — applied to competitor pricing pages, omitted on Google policy / vendor docs. Exactly right.
- **Sitemap priority weighting.** Reflects actual SEO value, not arbitrary defaults.
- **Heading hierarchy on every page.** No skipped levels, no decorative H2s, descriptive text.
- **No accidental noindex.** No accidental localhost canonicals. No broken sitemap entries.
