About A Happy Nails & Lashes

A Happy Nails & Lashes provides high-quality services. The staff does a great job with nail designs, including acrylic French Manicure and solar nail fill. They also do nice pedis and hybrid eye lashes, especially on Asian eyes. The owner is excellent and takes pride in her work, with attention to detail and precision. Customers highly recommend A Happy Nails & Lashes for mani/pedi, wax, and lash extensions, and appreciate the great prices and long-lasting results. With years of experience and satisfied customers, A Happy Nails & Lashes is a top choice for nail and lash services.
