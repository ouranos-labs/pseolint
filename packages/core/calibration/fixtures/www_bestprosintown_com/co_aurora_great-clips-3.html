About Great Clips

Great Clips in Aurora offers affordable haircuts for men, women, and kids. The salon provides various hair care services, including haircuts, beard trims, bang trims, and shampooing. Customers praise the staff for being knowledgeable, friendly, and skilled at giving great haircuts. The stylists are quick and efficient, often making the experience enjoyable with fun conversations. The salon is open evenings and weekends, with no appointment necessary, allowing walk-ins or online check-in to avoid wait times. Many clients appreciate the ability to use coupons and the consistent quality of service provided by the staff.
