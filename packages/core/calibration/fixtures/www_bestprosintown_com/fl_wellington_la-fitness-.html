About LA Fitness

LA Fitness is a top-notch gym that offers many amenities at an outstanding value. Features include state-of-the-art equipment, basketball, group fitness classes, a pool, saunas, and personal training. The Wellington location stands out for its friendly staff. The fantastic sauna is hot, but the pool is not very clean. Some of the machines are broken and have surface rust, with areas of chipped paint and torn rubber hand grips. Maintenance should be routine. Overall, it is very clean and provides a fantastic fitness experience, making it a recommended choice for anyone looking to reach their health goals.
