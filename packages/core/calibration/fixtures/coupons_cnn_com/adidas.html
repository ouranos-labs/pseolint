Cut costs on the Sales Page

Take a look at the "Sales Page" on Adidas.com and save up to 50% on everyday products. New items are added to the page regularly, so be sure to check back often. You can even add an adidas promo code to maximize your savings.

Military discount for 30% off

Are you a member of the military? Then don't leave this page without factoring in your discount, which is a full 30% off. All you need to do is verify your status with ID.me. Now, the item you wanted that didn't have a discount has a discount!

Secure rewards wins

Join adiClub rewards for free shipping immediately. Keep buying at adidas and you'll inevitably reach level 2 with 1,000-3,999 points. At this point, you'll receive an adidas discount code as a birthday gift!

15% off adidas coupon for your first order

Don't forget to sign up with your email and receive 15% off your order. Scroll to the bottom of the homepage to subscribe. You will be sent a unique single-use 15% Off 2024 adidas coupon by email for your first purchase. Be sure to use it quickly, as some tend to expire within 24 hours.

Free returns online or in-store

adidas offers free returns on products. Just be sure to return your items within 30-days with the original parts and packaging. There is no charge to ship the product back, and you’ll receive a full refund. You cannot return My-adidas custom items as you have personalized them to suit your taste.
