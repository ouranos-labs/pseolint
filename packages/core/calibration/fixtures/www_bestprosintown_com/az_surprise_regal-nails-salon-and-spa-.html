About Regal Nails, Salon & Spa

Regal Nails, Salon & Spa in Surprise, AZ, is committed to providing exceptional service and healthy results. The team takes great care with clients' nails, from manicures to pedicures, and provides a welcoming atmosphere. Clients praise the staff's professionalism, friendliness, and attention to detail. The salon uses high-quality products and offers a range of services, including foot massages. Even first-time customers receive a warm welcome and excellent treatment, with attention to detail and gentle care.
