Best Buy: Questions & Answers

Does Best Buy Have Free Delivery?

Best Buy offers free standard and next-day shipping on qualifying items of $35 or more and will be automatically taken off your order during checkout. Some items such as digital downloads, e-gift cards, special orders, and any items canceled do not qualify for free delivery.

Does Best Buy Offer a Sale Section?

You can save big on brand new products. Top-of-the-line products in new condition are priced to clear at value prices in their sale section, so make sure you check that out. You might also find a compatible coupon on this very website.

Are there Best Buy App Discounts?

Best Buy has an app that you can download on your mobile device. You can reserve products online, then pick up and pay in store. You get those added benefits of being able to shop anywhere you are, and you may also set sale notifications. As soon as discounts become available, you can be among the first to know. 

Does Best Buy Offer Special Discounts?

Best Buy offers students 10-30% off on laptops, desktops, cell phones, small appliances, computer accessories, and more. Sign up for their Student Deals program for the latest offers and discount codes. Best Buy doesn’t offer a senior discount. However, you can save at Best Buy by becoming a member of their rewards program, signing up to their email list, or browsing through the Best Buy coupon page available here at SFGate. You might also be eligible for a military or Geek Squad discount, but to confirm offers and eligibility, visit their website and speak with a customer service representative.

Does Best Buy Have a Rewards Program?

Become a member of the My Best Buy Rewards program and take advantage of exclusive offers such as free shipping on all orders, early access to sales and events, and collect reward points on all purchases. Earn points on every dollar you spend and redeem your Best Buy Rewards points on future purchases. Visit their website and create an account today to start saving big on your next order.

Does Best Buy Have a Customer Service Team?

Visit their customer service webpage to get answers to frequently asked questions, and submit a ticket in their contact form for any questions you may have. They are always delighted to hear from their happy customers so get in touch with any questions, comments or concerns you may have.

Does Best Buy Offer Refunds or a Price Match Guarantee?

For most products, you could qualify for free returns or exchanges within 30 days of purchase. To confirm eligibility, visit their site or contact customer service. Best Buy will also price match another competitor within 30 days of purchase. That’s a guarantee that you can trust.

Does Best Buy Offer Financing?

You can feel confident knowing that you can buy now and pay when you need to. You can buy now, and pay later with options for 3, 6, 12, or even 24-month financing. Spread the cost into easy payments. Additionally, you can browse this website to find that perfect Best Buy Coupon.

The Best Buy trade-in program is great if you want to upgrade technology that’s still in good condition. You don’t have to feel like you’re being wasteful for upgrading your Best Buy TV; you'll also save on a brand-new device. Out with the old and in with the new has never been easier.

Easy Returns

You can return items in three different ways: in-store, by mail carrier of your choice, or by prepaid UPS label. All you need is proof of purchase.

One criterion all Best Buy purchases have for returns is that they need to be in good condition.
